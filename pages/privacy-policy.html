Privacy Policy

Welcome to text2tool.in. We are committed to providing free, high-quality tools while maintaining a transparent relationship with our users regarding their data and privacy.

1. Advertising & Monetization

To keep our tools 100% free for everyone, we display advertisements on our website.

  • Third-Party Vendors: Vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Personalized Ads: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-out: Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

2. Data Processing & API Usage

We use a hybrid processing model to handle your requests:

  • Client-Side: Many tools (like basic PDF/Image formatting) process data directly in your browser. No data is sent to our servers.
  • Server-Side/API: For advanced tools (AI Generation, OCR, Image-to-Text), we use secure external APIs. By using these, you acknowledge that your data may be processed by these third-party services. We do not store this data permanently.

3. AI Content & User Responsibility

text2tool.in does not monitor, review, or store the prompts you enter or the results generated by our AI tools (Images, Audio, Characters). The input and the resulting output are the sole responsibility of the user. We are not liable for any copyright or ethical issues arising from user-generated content.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use LocalStorage to save your session data (like Temp Mail inbox) and Cookies for site analytics and advertising purposes. This helps us understand user behavior and provide a better experience.

5. Service Limits

While most of our tools are free and unlimited, we reserve the right to implement rate limits or temporary restrictions to prevent abuse and ensure service stability for all users.
