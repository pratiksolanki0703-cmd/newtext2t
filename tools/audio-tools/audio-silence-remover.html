Frequently Asked Questions

How to remove silence from audio automatically?

Upload your audio, set the silence threshold (dB), and click process. The tool will automatically detect and remove silent parts from your file.

What is the best dB setting for silence removal?

For voice recordings, -35dB to -45dB works best. Lower values detect more silence, while higher values keep softer sounds.

Does this tool reduce audio quality?

No. The tool only removes silent parts without affecting the original sound quality.

Can I remove silence from MP3 and WAV files?

Yes, this tool supports both MP3 and WAV audio formats.
